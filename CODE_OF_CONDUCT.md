# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, be respectful, assume good intent.
- No harassment, trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- No publishing others' private information.
- Stay on topic: this repo is about open source games and game-making resources.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning or a temporary/permanent ban from the
project, at the maintainers' discretion. Report issues by opening a private
contact channel with the maintainers (see the repo profile) — reports are
kept confidential.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
